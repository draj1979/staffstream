How it works

From org chart to AI workforce, in five steps.

This takes the place of a traditional org chart — except it's live, operational, and every step keeps a human in the loop.

01

Set up your organization

Admin registers the company, creates departments, defines KPIs, and creates roles with reporting lines.

02

Assign AI assistants

Each role gets a virtual employee slot. Admin assigns it to a real employee by email — the platform auto-provisions the assistant.

03

Employees meet their assistant

Employees log in, set their working KPIs, see what their assistant can do, and start delegating tasks immediately.

04

Assistants get to work

Assistants use their skills to complete tasks and reach out to other departments' assistants when needed — anything consequential pauses for approval.

05

Managers stay in the loop

A manager's AI assistant aggregates progress from the team's assistants and flags what needs attention.

FAQ

Questions enterprise buyers actually ask.

Is this replacing my employees with AI? +
No. Every AI assistant is paired with a human employee and works under their direction. StaffStream augments your team — it doesn't replace it.
How does StaffStream prevent AI assistants from accessing data they shouldn't? +
Every department has a data classification level, and every inter-assistant communication is checked against your company's policies before it happens. Sensitive data sharing requires human approval by default.
Can AI assistants act completely on their own? +
Not by default. New assistants start in a fully supervised mode requiring approval for consequential actions. Over time, admins can grant more autonomy for routine, low-risk tasks — always reversible.
Where is our data stored? +
All data is hosted in AWS's Mumbai region, aligned with India's DPDP Act for data residency and protection.
How do AI assistants connect to our internal systems? +
Through a secure, standardized connection method. Credentials are stored centrally and never exposed directly to the AI.
What if a skill we need doesn't exist? +
Admins or employees can create custom skills through a guided builder, or request new integrations through the platform's connection registry.
