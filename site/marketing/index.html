FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

Do I need an existing marketing team to work with Staffstream? +
No. Staffstream is built for businesses without an internal marketing team, or with a very small one. We act as your outsourced marketing team, handling strategy, content, social media, advertising and lead engagement.
Which social media platforms do you manage? +
It depends on your target audience and business objectives — commonly Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. We recommend platforms based on where your customers actually are, rather than assuming every business needs every platform.
How does the WhatsApp automation work? +
When a prospect responds to an ad or messages your WhatsApp channel, the bot welcomes them, answers common questions, collects requirements, asks qualification questions and captures lead information — then hands off to your sales team when a conversation needs a human.
Can you guarantee a certain number of leads or sales? +
We don't guarantee a specific number of leads, sales or revenue unless it's an explicit contractual commitment. We focus on building and continuously optimizing a marketing operation designed to generate relevant leads.
What information do you need before recommending a plan? +
We start by understanding your business — what you sell, your target customers and location, whether you're B2B or B2C, your current online presence and advertising activity, how leads are currently handled, and your primary marketing objective. Only then do we recommend the right combination of services.
What if I need pricing, contract terms or a custom proposal? +
Message us on WhatsApp or book a free demo — a Staffstream representative will walk you through pricing, contract terms and a proposal tailored to your business.
